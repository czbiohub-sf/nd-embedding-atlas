---
title: nd-embedding-atlas
description: Explore and annotate n-dimensional microscopy images with precomputed embeddings.
---

nd-embedding-atlas links precomputed embeddings to their source n-dimensional (`TCZYX`) microscopy images. Researchers can review, select, and annotate examples for downstream model training without uploading the dataset.

nd-embedding-atlas does not train models or include model-training code. Generate embeddings before launch and store them in a supported AnnData or MuData Zarr input.

<Callout type="warn" title="Active development">
  APIs, file-format support, and shipped presets may change between releases. Check the [supported
  formats](/supported-formats) before preparing production data.
</Callout>

## Start here

1. [Install ndea](/install) on macOS or Linux.
2. [Check the browser and system requirements](/requirements).
3. [Launch ndea](/launch) with an AnnData Zarr store or project YAML.
4. [Confirm that your data matches the supported contracts](/supported-formats).

```bash
ndea view path/to/data.zarr
```

Open `http://localhost:5055` in Chrome or Edge.
